Dealers and installers

What a KOOR dealer gets

  • Spec sheets and quick-start guides you can hand to a customer
  • Plug-by-plug wiring guides for the inverter and battery pairings you sell
  • Tech support from people who install this equipment themselves
  • Warranty handled in Goshen, not overseas
  • Stock on the floor — most orders ship within days
