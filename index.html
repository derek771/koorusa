Why KOOR

We install this equipment on dairies, shops and homes around northern Indiana. What we sell is what we would put in our own barn.

Split phase, done right

120/240 V out of every inverter, from 8 to 15 kW, with real surge for well pumps and compressors.

Grade-A lithium

16 kWh packs with active balancing and heater pads, in a cabinet on casters or a stack you carry in by the section.

10-year warranty

Ten years on the All-in-One and both batteries, five on the 12K off-grid, backed by Fantastech in Goshen, Indiana.

Support from installers

Plug-by-plug wiring guides, quick-starts and a phone number that reaches someone who has hung one of these.
