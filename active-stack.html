What it is

The Active Stack is the same 16 kWh Grade-A LiFePO4 pack as the 16K Pro, split into sections that stack on a base under a control head. Each section is a two-man carry through a doorway, which matters in a basement or a loft.

Every tower has a built-in precharge circuit that soft-starts the inverter on its own — no precharge button to remember. Active balancing moves charge between cells with a 2 A balance current, and heater pads protect the pack from sub-zero charging.

Up to 15 towers run on one communication chain with automatic addressing. Series connection is not allowed; towers always run in parallel.

Questions we get

How many towers can I run together?

Up to 15 on one communication chain, master at address 1. On a job with more than one inverter, give each inverter its own equal bank of towers.

Can I stack towers in series for a higher voltage?

No. Series connection is prohibited. Towers run in parallel only, on a 48 V system.

What is the precharge for?

An inverter's capacitors pull a big inrush when they first see the battery. The Active Stack soft-starts that connection itself with a resistor and time-delay relay, so there is no precharge button to press and no arc on the terminals.

Does every tower need a base and a control head?

Yes — one base and one control head per tower, whatever the section count.

Which inverters does it pair with?

Closed-loop with the KOOR All-in-One and 12K, and with major brands over CAN or RS485. We publish plug-by-plug wiring guides — ask for yours.

Is it UL listed?

No. Tell your inspector and insurer before specifying it on a permitted job.
